KPI Targets & Thresholds

Set management benchmarks used by the dashboard to classify performance as Green, Amber or Red.

Targets are stored in this browser and can be adjusted to Accucare's agreed service standards. For “lower is better” measures such as waiting time, no-show rate and stock-outs, performance is green when the value is at or below the target.
IndicatorDirectionGreen targetAmber toleranceUnit

Department KPI Targets

Define monthly service-volume, revenue and productivity targets for each outpatient department.

Each department can be managed against three core benchmarks: service volume, monthly revenue, and revenue per service. Green means the target is met or exceeded; Amber means performance is within the configured tolerance; Red indicates a material shortfall.
